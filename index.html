Bill of Materials (BOM)

  • 1x Arduino Uno R3: Synchronous 4-bit binary counter cycling 0000 to 1111 on D4–D7.
  • 1x 7404 (Hex Inverter): Generates inverted inputs A', B', C', D'.
  • 7421 (Dual 4-Input AND): Minterm decoders for 4-variable product terms.
  • 7411 (Triple 3-Input AND): Computes shared terms like ABC, BCD', AC'D.
  • 7408 (Quad 2-Input AND): Computes shared term AB for center star X13.
  • 7432 (Quad 2-Input OR): Sums product terms for multi-minterm outputs.
  • 25x LEDs: High-efficiency 5mm LEDs arranged in 5x5 symmetrical configuration.
  • 25x 220Ω Resistors: Individual current limiters for balanced illumination.

Optimization Highlight

By leveraging shared sub-expressions across multiple outputs—such as ABCD' (shared across X2, X3, X4, X6, X8), ABC'D (shared across X9, X11, X12), and ABC (shared across X1 and X5)—the total IC count was minimized while preserving exact pattern fidelity.
